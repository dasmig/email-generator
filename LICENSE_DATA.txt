Third-party data in resources/. Each dataset, its columns and how it is built
are described in doc/data.md.

countries.tsv
  Country codes and populations copied from country-generator.

providers.tsv
  Seeded from the free_email_domains of the locales of Faker
  (https://github.com/joke2k/faker), MIT licence; every weight, the global
  webmail shares and the extra domains are curated for this library.

fold.tsv
  Built from Unicode decomposition of the Latin blocks (Unicode Character
  Database) plus a few curated letters.

words.tsv
  Word lists copied from nickname-generator (same author and licence).

particles.tsv, profiles.tsv, email_styles.tsv
  Curated for this library.
